## 📌 About the Application

This web application is the online portal and **Interactive Stamp Designer Studio** for **N.P Enterprises**, Nagpur's premier specialist in custom rubber stamps, self-inking seals, paper embossers, business cards, flyers, and office stationery.

---

## ✨ Features

### 🛠️ Interactive Stamp Designer Studio
- **Dynamic SVG Impression Engine**: Real-time vector rendering with curved text arcs (`<textPath>`) for top and bottom curved text around circular and oval seal frames.
- **Realistic Wet Ink Bleed Filter**: Custom SVG turbulence & displacement filter that replicates authentic wet ink grain and paper impression edges.
- **5 Stamp Shapes**:
  - 🔘 **Circle**: Standard company registration seal and round office stamps.
  - ⬭ **Oval**: Advocate, legal practitioner, and notary seals.
  - ▭ **Rectangle**: Office address, signature, medical doctor, and status box stamps.
  - ▢ **Square**: Monograms, QR codes, and ornate framed stamps.
  - 🏵️ **Badge**: Serrated quality certificate and official emblem seals.

### 📋 6 One-Click Format Presets
- **Round Company Seal**: Standard company registration seal.
- **Advocate / Legal Seal**: Official legal practitioner oval layout.
- **Medical Doctor Stamp**: Name, qualifications, registration number, and medical cross emblem.
- **PAID / APPROVED Status Box**: Bold status box with date line.
- **Signature & Designation**: Official signatory block with divider line.
- **Notary Public Seal**: State notary public seal format with crown emblem.

### 🎨 Deep Customization Controls
- **Ink Palette**: Stamp Red (`#B23A2E`), Royal Blue (`#1D4ED8`), Jet Black (`#18181B`), Emerald Green (`#15803D`), Violet (`#7E22CE`), plus a native custom color picker.
- **Border Frame Styles**: Double Line, Solid Thick Border, Dashed/Dotted Edge, Serrated Postage Edge.
- **Emblem Selector**: Star (★), Scales of Justice (⚖), Medical Cross (✚), Verified Badge (✔), Crown (👑), Date Block (📅), or None.
- **Hand-Stamp Realism Sliders**: Tilt angle (-15° to +15°) and Ink Texture Distress (0% to 100%).
- **Mount Hardware Selector**: Self-inking mount, basic wooden rubber stamp, pocket portable stamp, pre-inked flash stamp, or paper embosser.

### 📱 Seamless WhatsApp Order Integration
- **Custom Order Cart**: Stores customized stamp specifications (Shape, Ink color, Text lines, Mount type).
- **Direct WhatsApp Messaging**: Pre-formats order details and sends them directly to `+91 98231 11814`.
- **Fulfillment Options**: Pickup from shop or Home / Office delivery in Nagpur (*delivery charges apply depending on location*).
- **Vector SVG Export**: Download custom stamp draft as an `.svg` file.
